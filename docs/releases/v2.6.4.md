# Zebby 2.6.4

- Atlassian listings now import the role, team and individual locations, along with responsibilities, qualifications, pay and benefits when listed.
- When a job site such as Uber requires browser verification, Zebby explains how to open the listing and paste its description. **Go to description** takes you to the field.
- Plan and Applications show import errors in a tinted banner above the form. Settings uses compact inline errors with **Retry** where available.
- Saving no longer shows a “Saved” label in the corner. Screen readers still announce the save.
- The credit pack picker stays open when you switch between locked models in Settings.
