# Zebby 2.7.2

- Fixes credit reconnection after reinstalling Zebby or switching between Microsoft Store and standalone installations. A fresh pairing or recovery code can reconnect a previously disconnected computer.
- Hides disconnected balances and usage bars in Settings, Credits and devices, and the sidebar. They return after a successful reconnection and balance refresh.
- Preserves wallet credentials during failed or interrupted recovery. Existing protections remain in place when switching away from a connected wallet with purchased credits.
- Stops automatic refresh attempts for disconnected wallets and removes technical IPC prefixes from error messages.
