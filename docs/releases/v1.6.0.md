# Zebby v1.6.0

A warmer workspace for planning your next move.

- Ember colors across Plan, Applications and Settings, in light and dark appearance.
- A compact application list with a role inspector for match evidence, resume, locations and notes. Details expand inline in smaller windows.
- Plan pairs the job posting with Your CV and keeps Save within reach. ATS keywords and resume themes remain editable side by side, with job importance percentages and copy buttons.
- Cancel analysis while it runs. Source inputs are protected, and cancellation keeps previous saved results.
- Cleaner Settings, shorter copy, consistent field sizes and clear keyboard focus.
- App-local New, Save and Search shortcuts, plus editing Undo.
- Thin Windows scrollbars widen during interaction. Mac keeps native scrolling conventions.

The complete New Application form retains its fields and behavior. Database format is unchanged.

Download the Windows x64 or ARM64 `.exe`, or Apple Silicon `.dmg`. Existing installations can use **Update**, then **Restart to update**.
