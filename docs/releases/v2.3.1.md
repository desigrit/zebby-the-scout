# Zebby 2.3.1

## Follow your Plan analysis

- A compact status line rolls smoothly beneath Analyze, with an elapsed timer.
- Preparing analysis, Waiting for model, Thinking, Generating results, and Saving results follow actual progress. Available stages depend on the provider.
- Ollama and downloaded models report thinking and answer generation as they happen. Thinking appears only when reasoning tokens arrive.
- Reduce Motion keeps status text still. Cancelling, retrying, or receiving an interrupted response clears the status without replacing previous results.

## Updating

In Settings, choose **Check for updates**, download 2.3.1, then choose **Restart to update**. Direct installers are also available below for Windows x64, Windows ARM64, and Apple Silicon Macs.

Your database, resumes, models, settings, and credits are preserved. No database or service configuration changes are required.
