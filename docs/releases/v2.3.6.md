# Zebby 2.3.6

- Plan now has **Move to Applied →** beside Save plan. It saves your edits and opens the job in Applications with today's date and Applied status.
- Job details, the saved listing, resume, match score and its explanation carry over without another analysis or credit charge.
- Your saved Plan keeps its keywords, resume themes and tailored overview. Jobs already in Applications open their existing record without creating duplicates or replacing its data.
