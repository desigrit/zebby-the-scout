# Zebby 2.3.4

- Analyze starts with your saved model and thinking level, without a credit review popup. Balance checks and spending limits still apply.
- The sidebar shows a slim usage bar with credits used, total credits and percentage used. Collapsed navigation shows the percentage and bar.
- Click either usage bar to buy more credits, whichever analysis method you use.
- Cancel remains available while a credit check is preparing. A late response cannot start a cancelled analysis.

Your database, resumes, settings and credit balance are preserved.
