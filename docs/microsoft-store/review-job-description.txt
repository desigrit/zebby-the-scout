Senior Product Manager
Northstar | Search & discovery
Seattle, WA or Remote

About the role
Help people discover products that fit their needs. Partner with design, engineering, and research to turn customer insight into a clear product strategy.

What you will do
* Lead customer discovery and define the product roadmap.
* Build and test new search and recommendation experiences.
* Set meaningful success metrics and evaluate experiments.
* Bring cross-functional teams together around customer outcomes.
* Explain product decisions clearly to partners and leadership.

What you bring
* Five or more years of product management experience.
* Experience delivering consumer products from discovery to launch.
* Strong written communication and sound product judgment.
* A track record of using research and data to make decisions.
* Experience working closely with design, engineering, and analytics.
